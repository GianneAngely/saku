# Code of conduct

Saku is a friendly little project. Please keep it that way.

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Swift or to open source.
- Talk about the work, not the person. Disagree with ideas politely and give reasons.
- No harassment, insults, or unwelcome attention of any kind, in any space linked to this project.
- Respect people's time: search issues before opening a new one, and keep threads on topic.

If someone is making the space unpleasant, report it privately through the repository's Security tab ("Report a vulnerability") or by contacting the maintainer on GitHub. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads or block people who don't follow these rules.
